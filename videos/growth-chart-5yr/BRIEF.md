## Intent

A clean, corporate bar-chart animation: 5 bars rise year by year across 30
seconds, each with a counting number landing in sync above it. Minimal white
background, modern blue (bars) + green (growth accent) palette. Subtle,
metronomic pacing that reads as musical without any actual audio track or
decorative motion. Presentation-ready — nothing competes with the data.

## Customizations

- Dynamic count-up numbers above each bar, synced 1:1 to that bar's rise
  (same duration/ease, so number and bar land as one beat).
- Understated visual rhythm: evenly-spaced beats + a small landing pulse on
  each bar/number pair, standing in for "background music cues" — no audio
  track requested, so none is added.

## Notes

- No real dataset was supplied. Using an illustrative revenue-growth series
  ($1.0M -> $2.8M, ~28% YoY) — swappable placeholder, flagged to the user.
- No narration, no logo, no extra chrome — scope stays exactly the ask.
