## Intent

A launch/promo video for Linear (linear.app), selling the product (not a
neutral site tour). Energetic but minimal — matches Linear's own design
ethos. Structure is fixed by the user: a hook stating the problem, three
feature beats each with a real UI screenshot and a one-line caption, and an
end card with the logo and a "Try it free" CTA.

## Customizations

- Use the site's own captured palette, type, and real UI screenshots as the
  visual source (not invented mockups).
- Exactly 3 feature beats, each: one UI capture + one-line caption.
- End card: logo + "Try it free".
- Narration: female voice, confident tone (HeyGen "Marcia" default fits;
  confirm at Step 3.1).
- Music: subtle electronic BGM bed, mixed under -18 dB (LUFS/gain target
  noted for the audio step — quieter than the pipeline's usual bed level so
  it stays under the VO at all times).

## Notes

- Sweet spot for this route is 30-90s; 45s requested fits comfortably.
- No stock-photo aesthetic — real product screenshots only.
